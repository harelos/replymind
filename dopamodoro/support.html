Common questions

My streak reset. Why?

Streaks reset when more than one day passes between focus sessions. Pro users get 3 Streak Freezes per month — these auto-apply to bad days so the streak survives. If you're on the free tier and want streak protection, consider Pro.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on the device where Dopamodoro is installed. We have no servers and cannot see your data. Uninstalling removes everything. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel Pro?

Android: Open Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Dopamodoro → Cancel subscription.

Chrome extension: Use the Paddle billing portal from your purchase email, or email support with your purchase email and we will help cancel it.

I'm on Chrome. Is there an Android app?

Yes, Android coming soon to Google Play. iOS will follow.

Can I export my data?

Chrome extension: yes — Journal → Export downloads a JSON file. Android: coming in a future version.

I want a feature added.

Email us, or use the in-app "Send feedback" feature. We read every request. The ADHD founder building this app uses it daily and cares deeply about making it better.

The AI Coach isn't responding.

The AI Coach (Chrome Pro feature) requires you to paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings. Make sure the key is valid and your OpenAI account has credit. We do not manage AI on your behalf at this time.
