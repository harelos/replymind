Questions

interview thank you email builder FAQ

When is the best time to send a post-interview thank-you email?

Send your thank-you email within 24 hours of the interview, ideally by the end of the same business day.

Should I send separate thank-you emails to each interviewer?

Yes. Tailor each email individually to reference specific points discussed with each interviewer.

What subject line works best for an interview thank-you email?

Use clear subjects like "Thank you - [Position Title] interview - [Your Name]".

What should I do if I forgot to mention something during the interview?

Use the thank-you email to briefly add relevant achievements or answer questions you felt under-explained.

Is email better than a handwritten thank-you note?

Email is preferred in modern hiring as decisions happen quickly; a handwritten note arrives too late.
