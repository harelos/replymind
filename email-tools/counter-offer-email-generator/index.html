Questions

counter offer email generator FAQ

Will an employer rescind a job offer if I send a counter offer email?

Rescissions are extremely rare when counter offers are polite, data-backed, and framed around enthusiasm for the role.

What percentage above the original offer should I counter with?

Countering 5% to 15% above the baseline offer is standard across tech, finance, and corporate leadership roles.

Should I counter over email or over the phone?

Email provides a documented paper trail and gives hiring managers the precise numbers they need for HR approvals.

Can I negotiate benefits like remote work or extra PTO instead of salary?

Yes. Non-salary perks like sign-on bonuses, remote flexibility, or flexible start dates are highly negotiable.

What subject line should I use for salary negotiation emails?

Use simple subjects like "Job Offer - [Position Title] - [Your Name]" or reply in the existing offer thread.
