Expert Tip

Always negotiate base salary first before turning to sign-on bonuses or equity. Base compensation permanently anchors future raises, bonuses, and severance calculations.

The V.A.L.U.E. Salary Framework (Validation, Anchor, Leverage, Understanding, Execution)

The V.A.L.U.E. Framework ensures salary conversations remain collaborative. Start with Validation of the offer, state your Anchor compensation figure, highlight your professional Leverage, show Understanding of budget constraints, and outline clear Execution next steps.

Questions

Salary Negotiation FAQ

Is it acceptable to negotiate salary via email?

Yes. Email provides a documented baseline, gives HR time to consult compensation benchmarks, and reduces real-time pressure.

How much higher should my counteroffer be?

Countering 10% to 15% above the initial offer is standard industry practice when supported by experience and market benchmarks.

Can an employer rescind an offer for negotiating?

Rescinding is rare if your negotiation is polite, backed by market data, and framed around mutual long-term success.

What if the hiring manager says the salary is fixed?

Explore non-base compensation such as signing bonuses, additional equity grants, early performance reviews, or remote flexibility.

When should I send a salary negotiation email?

Send your counteroffer within 24 to 48 hours of receiving the official written offer letter.
