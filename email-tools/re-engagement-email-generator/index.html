Questions

re-engagement email generator FAQ

When should a re-engagement email sequence be triggered?

Trigger re-engagement sequences when a subscriber has not opened or clicked an email in 60 to 90 days.

Why is list pruning important for email deliverability?

Removing unengaged subscribers improves open rates, reduces spam complaints, and protects sender domain reputation.

What subject lines work best for re-engagement emails?

Subject lines with curiosity or direct questions work best, such as 'Are we still on your radar?', 'We miss you!', or 'Should we unsubscribe you?'.

What percentage of dormant subscribers can be reactivated?

Targeted re-engagement campaigns successfully rescue 10% to 14% of dormant email subscribers before list cleaning.

Should unengaged subscribers be deleted automatically if they don't respond?

Yes. Subscribers who do not interact after a 3-part re-engagement sequence should be unsubscribed or suppressed.

What incentives encourage subscriber re-activation?

Exclusive free ebooks, 20% discount codes, free month extensions, or streamlined email preference management options.
