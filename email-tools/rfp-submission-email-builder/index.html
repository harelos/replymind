Questions

rfp submission email builder FAQ

What documents should be attached to an RFP submission email?

Attach executive summary, technical proposal, pricing sheet, SOC2 compliance certs, and client case studies.

How early before the deadline should an RFP submission email be sent?

Send your submission at least 2 to 4 hours before the strict cut-off deadline to avoid technical delivery errors.

What subject line works best when submitting an RFP proposal?

Use precise subjects like "RFP Submission: [Project Name] - [RFP #] - [Your Company Name]".

Should pricing details be stated in the email body or attached?

State total contract estimate range in the email cover letter while attaching detailed line-item cost schedules.

Who should sign the RFP submission email cover letter?

The Enterprise Account Executive or VP of Sales should sign formal RFP cover submissions.
