Questions

cold call follow up email FAQ

How quickly should I send an email after a cold call?

Send the follow-up email within 15 to 30 minutes of ending the phone call or leaving a voicemail while the context is fresh.

Should I send a follow-up email if I left a voicemail?

Yes. A voicemail followed immediately by a concise email increases response rates by up to 28% across B2B sales cycles.

What subject line works best for cold call follow-up emails?

Use simple, non-gimmicky subjects like "Tried reaching you earlier - [Company]" or "Following up on my voicemail".

What should I do if the prospect said they were busy on the call?

Acknowledge their tight schedule, summarize your value proposition in two sentences, and propose a specific time next week.

How many times should I follow up after an initial cold call?

Maintain a sequence of 3 to 4 multi-channel touchpoints over 10 business days before resting the lead.
