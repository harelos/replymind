A reliable structure

What makes this message work

Restore context

Mention the proposal, meeting, invoice, or decision so the reader knows what this is about.

Add a reason to reply

Share an update, answer a likely objection, or propose a specific next action.

Make the response small

A yes/no question or two time options gets more replies than an open-ended request.

Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

“Just checking in” asks for attention but contributes nothing. A useful follow-up reminds the recipient why the conversation matters and reduces the effort needed to continue it.

Timing depends on the situation: one or two business days for an urgent operational question, roughly a week for a proposal, and longer for a relationship that is not time-sensitive.

follow up email generator FAQ

When should I send a follow-up email?

For most professional conversations, wait two to five business days unless a deadline requires an earlier response.

How many follow-ups are appropriate?

Two or three thoughtful follow-ups are reasonable in many business situations. Stop when the recipient declines or asks for space.
