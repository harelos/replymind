## What makes this message work

Express gratitude

Acknowledge their service and dedication during their employment tenure.

Emphasize confidentiality

Reassure the employee that feedback is anonymized and used for positive organization growth.

Offer flexible formats

Provide choices between a 1-on-1 virtual call or an anonymous online survey form.

Expert Tip

Always provide a survey alternative. HR data shows that offering an optional digital exit survey alongside a 1-on-1 interview invitation increases employee feedback completion rates by 41%.

Best practices for exit interview invitations

Offboarding is a critical phase of the employee lifecycle. How an organization treats departing colleagues impacts employer brand reputation, alumni networks, and future talent recruitment. Whether you are an HR Business Partner managing executive offboarding, a People Operations Specialist scheduling exit chats, an IT Admin coordinating equipment return, or a Department Head conducting handover reviews, thoughtful communication ensures positive offboarding experiences.

Data shows follow-ups sent within 48 hours increase response rates by 34%, while exit interviews conducted 3 to 5 days prior to an employee's final day yield 62% more actionable feedback. Highlighting confidentiality and organization improvement encourages departing employees to share genuine insights.

Questions

exit interview invitation email FAQ

Are exit interviews mandatory for departing employees?

No. Exit interviews are voluntary in most organizations, though HR teams strongly encourage participation.

How far in advance of an employee's last day should the invitation be sent?

Send the exit interview invitation 3 to 5 business days before their scheduled last working day.

Who should conduct the exit interview?

An HR representative or neutral third-party representative should conduct the interview rather than direct managers.

What if a departing employee declines the exit interview?

Respect their choice and offer an anonymous digital survey link as a low-friction alternative.

What subject line works best for exit interview invitations?

Use supportive subjects like "Exit Interview Invitation & Offboarding Info - [Employee Name]".
