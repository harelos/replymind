Questions

AI email writer in your voice FAQ

How many emails are needed to learn a writing style?

ReplyMind is engineered to extract a reliable writing voice profile from as few as 2 to 3 representative email samples. Providing samples across different recipient relationships improves profile nuance.

Does ReplyMind upload my sent mailbox to build the profile?

No. You paste selected writing samples manually, and style analysis occurs locally on your browser without uploading your mailbox to external servers.

Will every generated reply sound identical?

No. The style profile establishes baseline syntax and habits, while the active message content, recipient context, and selected intent dictate the unique substance of each reply.

How does voice matching differ from standard ChatGPT prompts?

Standard ChatGPT prompts rely on generic tone adjectives like 'professional' or 'friendly'. Voice-matched AI dynamically injects your exact opening habits, punctuation preferences, and structural rhythm.

Can I set up different voice profiles for clients vs internal colleagues?

Yes, ReplyMind allows tone and relationship adjustments so your voice naturally shifts formality based on whether you are emailing an executive client or a team member.

Is my personal writing voice data kept private?

Yes. All style profiles are stored locally in your browser storage and are never used to train public generative models.
