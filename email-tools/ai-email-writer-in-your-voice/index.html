Questions

AI email writer in your voice FAQ

How many emails are needed to learn a writing style?

ReplyMind is designed to start from two or three representative samples you choose. More varied samples can improve the profile.

Does ReplyMind upload my sent mailbox to build the profile?

No. You paste selected samples, and the style analysis is built on your device.

Will every reply be identical?

No. The style profile guides expression while the active message, intent, and contact context determine the content.
