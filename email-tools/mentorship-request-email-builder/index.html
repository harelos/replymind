Questions

mentorship request email builder FAQ

Should I ask someone to be my mentor in the very first email?

It is usually better to start with an informal 15-minute chat before proposing an ongoing formal mentorship.

How often should I ask to meet with a mentor?

Propose meeting once a month or once a quarter for 30 minutes to respect their professional workload.

What should I bring to a mentorship meeting?

Prepare 2 to 3 specific questions or challenges in advance and send an agenda at least 24 hours prior.

How can I offer value back to a mentor?

Share updates on how you implemented their advice, send relevant industry reports, or assist with projects when possible.

What subject line works best for requesting mentorship?

Use clear, humble subject lines such as "Seeking career guidance - [Your Name]" or "[Alumni Name] - Mentorship inquiry".
