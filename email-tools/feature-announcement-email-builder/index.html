Questions

feature announcement email builder FAQ

What makes a feature announcement email successful?

Successful feature emails focus on user productivity benefits rather than technical specs, show short UI GIFs, and provide deep links into the app.

How often should feature announcement emails be sent?

Send major feature updates monthly, and bundle smaller enhancements into a quarterly 'What's New' product digest.

Should feature announcement emails include animated GIFs?

Yes. Including 5-second animated UI GIFs increases feature click-through rates by over 30% by showing immediate visual clarity.

What subject lines drive high open rates for feature updates?

Subject lines focusing on capability gains perform best, such as 'New: Draft replies 3x faster with AI Voice Matching'.

How can product teams track feature announcement success?

Track email click-through rate, first-day feature activation count, and 30-day feature adoption retention.

Who should receive feature announcement emails?

Segment by user tier: send core updates to all active workspace users, and premium feature updates to targeted paid plan accounts.
