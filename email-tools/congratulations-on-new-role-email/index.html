Questions

congratulations on new role email FAQ

When should I send a congratulations email for a new role?

Send your email within 24 to 48 hours of hearing about their promotion or LinkedIn role update.

What should I write if a colleague is moving to a competing company?

Keep your message supportive, focus on their personal career growth, and offer to stay in touch.

Should I send congratulations on LinkedIn or email?

Email offers a personal touch for close colleagues, while LinkedIn works great for broader professional networks.

What subject line works best when congratulating someone on a new role?

Use enthusiastic subjects like "Congratulations on the promotion, [Name]!" or "So happy for your new role at [Company]!".

Can I send congratulatory emails to former bosses or clients?

Yes! Congratulating former managers or clients keeps valuable professional relationships active over time.
