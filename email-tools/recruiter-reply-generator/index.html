What makes this message work

State your interest level

Interested, curious, not now, or not a fit are all valid answers.

Ask screening questions

Move to one next step

Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

A recruiter message is not an obligation to interview. A short, professional response can preserve the relationship while protecting your time.

If declining, you may mention the kind of opportunity you would consider in the future—without writing a full career brief.

Interested

Questions

recruiter reply generator FAQ

Should I ask salary before a call?

Yes, when compensation is a meaningful constraint. It can save time for both sides.

How do I decline a recruiter politely?

Thank them, say the role or timing is not right, and optionally name what you would consider later.
