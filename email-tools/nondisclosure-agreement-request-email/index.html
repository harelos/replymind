Questions

nondisclosure agreement request email FAQ

Why should an NDA be signed before sharing product details?

An executed NDA legally protects proprietary IP, trade secrets, financial data, and customer information.

What is the difference between mutual and unilateral NDAs?

A mutual NDA protects confidential data shared by both parties, while a unilateral NDA protects one party's information.

Can I send an NDA via electronic signature links?

Yes. Sending DocuSign or e-signature links makes signing frictionless for external parties.

What subject line works best for NDA request emails?

Use formal subjects like "Mutual NDA Execution Request - [Company Name]" or "Standard NDA for Review".

Who should sign Non-Disclosure Agreements?

Authorized corporate officers, Legal Counsel, VPs, or Founders should execute formal NDAs.
