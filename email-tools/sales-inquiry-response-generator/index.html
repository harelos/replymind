Questions

sales inquiry response generator FAQ

How quickly should I answer a sales inquiry?

As quickly as your team can provide a useful, accurate answer. An immediate acknowledgement can help when research is required.

How many qualification questions should I ask?

Usually two or three in the first message. Ask only questions that change the recommendation.

Should every inquiry become a meeting?

No. Pricing, documentation, or a clear written answer may be a better next step.
