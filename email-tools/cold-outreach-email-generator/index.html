Cold Outreach FAQ

What is the ideal length for a B2B cold email?

Keep cold emails between 50 and 125 words. Short, focused emails achieve up to 3x higher response rates.

How do I increase cold email reply rates?

Personalize the opening line, focus on the recipient's pain point, and end with a low-friction question like 'Open to learning more?'

How many follow-ups should I send in a cold sequence?

A 3 to 4 touchpoint sequence spaced over 10 business days yields optimal conversion without triggering spam filters.

Should I include calendar links in initial cold emails?

No. Direct booking links increase spam filter flags and feel pushy in a first interaction.

What is a good subject line for cold outreach?

Short, informal, lower-case subject lines (2-4 words) like 'quick question re: onboarding' achieve the highest open rates.
