A webinar follow-up email is a post-event nurturing message sent to both session attendees and absent registrants to share event recordings, presentation decks, and next steps. Writing an effective follow-up email involves thanking attendees (or acknowledging absentees), embedding direct recording access links, highlighting key Q&A takeaways, and offering a secondary call-to-action such as a product demo or consultation. Best practice requires sending follow-ups within 24 hours of session end.

Best practices for converting leads with post-webinar follow-up emails

The true ROI of a webinar campaign is generated after the live event ends. Whether you are a Demand Generation Specialist nurturing B2B sales leads, a Marketing Operations Lead tracking event conversions, an Enterprise Account Executive following up with key accounts, or a Content Marketer repurposing video assets, structuring follow-up emails effectively maximizes lead conversion.

Nurture data shows that sending webinar recordings within 24 hours of live broadcast generates 47% higher replay view rates than delayed follow-ups. Prompt delivery captures user intent while session topics are top of mind.

To draft a high-converting webinar follow-up email, personalize the opening for live attendees ("Thank you for joining us!") versus absent registrants ("Sorry we missed you live!"). Provide direct links to the video recording and downloadable slide deck PDF, summarize 3 top Q&A highlights, and include a clear secondary call-to-action such as "Schedule a 1-on-1 product demo." Questions

webinar follow up email builder FAQ

When should post-webinar follow-up emails be sent?

Send follow-up emails within 4 to 24 hours after the live session ends while interest remains peak.

Should separate emails be sent to attendees versus non-attendees?

Yes. Tailor the opening: thank live attendees for their participation, and tell non-attendees 'Sorry we missed you—here is your replay link.'

What materials should be included in a webinar follow-up email?

Include the recording link, slide deck PDF download, Q&A summary, and a call-to-action for a product demo or consultation.

How do webinar follow-up emails drive sales leads?

Including a clear secondary CTA (such as 'Book a 1-on-1 demo with our strategy team') converts educated webinar attendees into sales pipeline.

How long do webinar replay links remain active?

Keep replay links active for at least 30 days, or host them permanently on a gated video landing page.

What open rate should be expected for webinar follow-up emails?

Webinar follow-up emails average 40% to 55% open rates because registrants actively anticipate receiving the recording link.
