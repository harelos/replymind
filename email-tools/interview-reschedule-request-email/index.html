Provide immediate notice

Alert the recruiter or hiring manager as soon as a legitimate conflict becomes known.

Keep reasons brief

State a general, professional reason like an urgent work obligation or illness without over-sharing.

The R.E.S.E.T. Framework (Regret, Explanation, Suggestions, Flexibility, Timeliness)

The R.E.S.E.T. Framework aids Talent Acquisition Leads, Senior Engineers, Executive Candidates, and Operations Specialists in handling schedule adjustments smoothly. Express initial Regret for inconvenience, offer a brief high-level Explanation, share time Suggestions, demonstrate total Flexibility, and deliver message with Timeliness.

Questions

interview reschedule request email FAQ

Will rescheduling an interview hurt my chances of getting hired?

Not if done professionally and promptly. Recruiters understand that urgent personal or professional conflicts happen.

How much advance notice should I give when rescheduling?

Give at least 24 hours advance notice whenever possible. For emergencies, notify them immediately.

Should I explain the exact reason why I need to reschedule?

Keep explanations brief and general (e.g. "an urgent personal obligation" or "an unavoidable scheduling conflict").

What subject line should I use when requesting an interview reschedule?

Reply directly in the existing interview calendar thread or use "Reschedule request: [Position] interview - [Your Name]".

How many time slots should I offer for the rescheduled interview?

Provide 3 to 4 distinct time slots across different days to give the interviewer maximum flexibility.
