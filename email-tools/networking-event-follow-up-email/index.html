Complete Guide

Best practices for networking event follow-ups

Networking is the bedrock of career growth and business development. However, exchanging business cards or scanning LinkedIn QR codes at a mixer is only the first step. Whether you are an Executive Assistant building vendor relationships, a Software Engineer expanding industry contacts, a Consultant meeting prospective partners, or a Founder connecting with potential advisors, a warm follow-up converts casual acquaintances into long-term professional allies.

Data shows follow-ups sent within 48 hours increase response rates by 34%, while messages containing a personalized topic reference achieve a 61% higher engagement rate than generic greetings. To maximize impact, avoid treating networking as a sales pitch. Approach outreach with curiosity, value sharing, and long-term perspective.

Questions

networking event follow up email FAQ

How soon after a networking event should I follow up?

Send your follow-up email within 24 to 48 hours while memories of the event conversation are fresh.

What should I write if I lost their business card?

Find them on LinkedIn or alumni directories, referencing the specific event panel or table where you spoke.

How do I avoid sounding like a transactional pitch?

Focus on mutual learning, shared interests, and offering value rather than asking for immediate favors.

What is a good subject line for networking follow-up emails?

Use natural subjects like "Great chatting at [Event Name]" or "Following up on our conversation at [Mixer]".

Should I connect on LinkedIn before or after emailing?

Sending a personalized email first or simultaneously with a custom LinkedIn invitation builds stronger rapport.
