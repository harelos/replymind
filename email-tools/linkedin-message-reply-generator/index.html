Questions

LinkedIn message reply generator FAQ

How long should a LinkedIn reply be?

Usually 30 to 100 words. Longer replies are appropriate only when the conversation requires detail.

Can I use the same reply for every recruiter?

You can reuse a structure, but the role, sender, and level of interest should change the content.

Does ReplyMind auto-send LinkedIn messages?

No. It drafts a message for your review and you decide whether to send it.
