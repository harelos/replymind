Questions

Promotion Request FAQ

When is the best time to ask for a promotion via email?

Request a promotion meeting 1 to 2 months prior to annual review cycles or immediately following a successful project completion.

How long should a promotion request email be?

Keep the email under 200 words. Use bullet points for key achievements and request an in-person or 1-on-1 meeting.

What should I bring to the promotion meeting?

Bring a 1-page document summarizing key metrics, peer testimonials, completed deliverables, and your proposed responsibilities.

How do I ask for a promotion if my manager is busy?

Frame the meeting around long-term team planning during your regular 1-on-1 check-in.

What if my promotion request is delayed due to budget?

Ask for clear, written milestones and a specific 3 to 6 month re-evaluation timeline.
