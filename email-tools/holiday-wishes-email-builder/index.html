Questions

holiday wishes email builder FAQ

When should corporate holiday emails be sent?

Send client holiday emails between December 10th and December 20th before key decision-makers go on leave.

How do I keep corporate holiday wishes inclusive?

Use inclusive greetings like "Warmest holiday wishes" or "Season's greetings" to respect diverse traditions.

Should I include business proposals in holiday emails?

No. Keep holiday emails focused strictly on gratitude, relationship building, and holiday hours.

What subject line works best for professional holiday greetings?

Use warm subjects like "Season's Greetings from [Company Name]" or "Happy Holidays & Thank You!".

Should holiday closure dates be included in the email?

Yes. Including office closure schedules helps clients plan their requests and emergency escalations.
