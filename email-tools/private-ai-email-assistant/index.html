A reliable structure

What makes this message work

Style profile stays local

Samples you deliberately paste are analyzed on your device to build the writing-style profile.

Contact notes are saved locally

A relevant note is transmitted only when you request that contact’s draft and is not stored on ReplyMind servers.

Active text is processed transiently

The message you ask ReplyMind to answer is sent through the generation API, then discarded rather than stored or used for training.

Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

The small template builder on this page runs entirely in your browser and sends no inputs anywhere. The extension’s AI generation is different: it needs the active message to produce a contextual draft.

ReplyMind never sends messages automatically. Optional product analytics require affirmative consent and exclude message text, generated replies, recipient identities, and contact notes.

Questions

private AI email assistant FAQ

Is ReplyMind completely offline?

No. Style profiling and saved contact notes are local, but requested AI generation sends the active message and relevant context to the generation service.

Does ReplyMind store email text?

ReplyMind states that message text is processed for generation and not stored or used to train models.

Does it send messages automatically?

No. ReplyMind creates a draft for your review; you remain responsible for sending.
