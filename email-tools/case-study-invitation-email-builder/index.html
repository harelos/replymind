The C.R.E.S.T. Framework provides Content Marketing Directors, Customer Advocacy Leads, Product Marketers, and PR Managers with a proven case study recruitment model. Best practices for inviting clients to participate in customer case studies

Questions

case study invitation email builder FAQ

How do you pitch a case study invitation to a busy client?

Lead with praise for their specific metrics, emphasize mutual PR exposure, guarantee minimal time investment (20-min interview), and confirm final editorial approval.

What time commitment should be requested in case study invitations?

Request a single 20-to-30-minute virtual interview session, with all drafting handled by your content marketing team.

Why should clients agree to participate in customer case studies?

Case studies offer valuable co-marketing backlinks, executive personal brand visibility, media PR reach, and industry thought-leadership positioning.

How can companies address client legal and privacy concerns?

Assure clients in writing that no sensitive financial data will be published without explicit approval from their legal/PR team.

When in the customer lifecycle should case study invitations be sent?

Send invitations after 60 to 90 days of successful onboarding or immediately following a proven KPI success milestone.

What materials should be prepared prior to the case study interview?

Send 4 to 5 interview questions in advance so executive interviewees can review metrics with their team beforehand.
