The R.E.C.A.P. Framework (Results, Execution, Commitments, Alignment, Progression)

The R.E.C.A.P. Framework helps Scrum Masters, Operations Directors, Account Executives, and Customer Success Managers craft high-impact follow-ups. First, summarize Results achieved in the conversation. Second, outline Execution steps agreed upon. Third, detail individual Commitments with assigned owners and deadlines. Fourth, prompt Alignment by requesting corrections. Fifth, define project Progression towards the next milestone.

Questions

meeting recap and action items FAQ

How quickly after a meeting should a recap email be sent?

Send the meeting recap within 24 hours of the meeting conclusion while discussions and decisions are fresh in all participants' minds.

Who is responsible for sending the meeting recap and action items?

Typically the meeting host, project manager, or designated note-taker is responsible for distributing the official recap email.

How should action items be formatted for maximum clarity?

Format action items as bullet points starting with the assignee's name in bold, followed by a clear verb, deliverable description, and exact due date.

What should I do if someone disagrees with a decision noted in the recap?

Invite attendees to reply directly within 24 hours if any details require correction, allowing minor misunderstandings to be resolved asynchronously.

Should meeting recaps be sent to stakeholders who could not attend?

Yes. Including absent key stakeholders keeps cross-functional teams aligned without requiring additional catch-up meetings.

How does a meeting recap differ from formal meeting minutes?

A meeting recap is a concise, action-focused summary designed for quick reading, whereas meeting minutes are formal, detailed legal records of discussions.
