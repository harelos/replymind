Questions

Resignation Email FAQ

How much notice should I give in a resignation email?

Two weeks (10 business days) is standard for most professional roles. Executive or specialized technical roles may require 30 days.

Should I state the reason for my departure?

You are not required to provide detailed reasons. Keeping the explanation high-level (e.g. 'pursuing a new opportunity') is best practice.

Who should be copied on a resignation email?

Send the email directly to your immediate manager and CC your HR representative.

Should I negotiate counteroffers in a resignation email?

No. A resignation email should be a definitive notice, not an opening bid for counteroffer negotiations.

Can a resignation email be sent on a Friday?

Yes, Friday afternoon or Monday morning are standard times to submit formal notice.
