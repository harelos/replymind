A reliable structure

What makes this message work

Name one owner

Shared ownership often becomes no ownership. Put one person in charge of the result.

Define done

Describe the deliverable or decision, not simply the activity to perform.

Set authority and checkpoints

Clarify what they can decide and when you want an update.

Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

Good delegation reduces future messages because the person knows the goal, boundaries, and point of escalation.

Avoid disguising delegation as a vague favor. Directness is respectful when responsibility and capacity are clear.

Questions

delegation email generator FAQ

What belongs in a delegation email?

Owner, outcome, context, deadline, authority, available resources, and the next check-in.

How do I delegate without sounding demanding?

Be direct about responsibility while showing trust and offering necessary support.

Should I copy the whole team?

Only people who need visibility or have a role in the outcome.
