The A.L.I.G.N. Framework (Announcement, Context, Impact, Guidance, Next steps)

The A.L.I.G.N. Framework aids CEOs, Chief Communications Officers, VPs of HR, and Operations Directors in executing clear broadcasts. State the main Announcement, establish strategic Context, detail employee Impact, offer clear Guidance, and outline immediate Next steps.

Questions

company announcement email builder FAQ

Who should send company-wide announcement emails?

CEOs, Founders, HR VPs, or Chief Communications Officers should send all-hands executive announcements.

When is the best time to send a major company announcement?

Send company announcements on Tuesday or Wednesday mornings between 9:00 AM and 10:30 AM for peak readability.

How long should a corporate company announcement email be?

Keep announcements under 300 words with clear headings, bullet points, and links to detailed documents.

How do I address potential employee concerns in an announcement?

Include a dedicated Q&A section or host a live company all-hands meeting following the email.

What subject line works best for company-wide announcements?

Use authoritative subjects like "[Company Update] Strategic Growth & Leadership Expansion" or "Important Announcement: Q4 Priorities".
