A reliable structure

What makes this message work

Name the failure

Say what went wrong without euphemisms that make responsibility disappear.

Acknowledge the impact

Show that you understand what the mistake required from the other person.

State the repair

Give the exact correction, owner, and timing whenever possible.

Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

“Sorry for any inconvenience” often feels empty because it avoids both the mistake and the impact. A concrete apology is usually shorter and more credible.

Do not mix the apology with a request for immediate forgiveness. The goal is to repair the situation first.

Delay

Missed deadline

I missed the Tuesday deadline and did not warn you early enough. The revised file will be with you tomorrow at 2 PM.

Error

Incorrect information

I sent the wrong pricing table. I have corrected the figures and attached the reviewed version here.

Service

Customer problem

We failed to update your shipping address in time. We are resending the order today at no cost.

Do

Keep these

  • Use “I” or “we” clearly
  • Name the corrective action
  • Follow through on the new promise
Avoid

Remove these

  • Say “if you felt”
  • Hide behind passive voice
  • Over-explain your intention
Questions

professional apology email generator FAQ

Should an apology email include an explanation?

Only if it helps the recipient understand the solution. An explanation should not become an excuse.

How do I apologize for a late reply?

Acknowledge the delay briefly, answer the message fully, and avoid a long story about why you were busy.

Can an apology be too long?

Yes. Long apologies can shift attention toward your feelings. Keep the focus on impact and repair.
