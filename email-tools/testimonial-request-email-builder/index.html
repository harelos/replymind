The S.P.A.R.K. Testimonial Framework (Show gratitude, Point to success, Ask for review, Provide prompts, Keep simple)

The S.P.A.R.K. Framework provides Marketing Managers, Client Success Leads, Agency Owners, and Product Marketing Specialists with an effective review collection model. First, Show gratitude. Second, Point to success. Third, Ask for review. Fourth, Provide prompts. Finally, Keep simple with a direct link.

Complete Guide

Best practices for requesting client testimonials and video reviews

Authentic customer social proof is one of the highest-converting assets in modern B2B marketing. Whether you are a Product Marketing Manager building landing page case studies, an Agency Owner gathering client quotes, a Customer Advocacy Lead collecting video reviews, or a Growth Marketer generating G2/Capterra reviews, asking at the right moment makes all the difference.

Conversion data demonstrates that supplying 3 guided questions in a testimonial request email boosts response completion rates by 52%. Busy executives want to help, but blank-page anxiety often delays their response.

To draft an effective testimonial request email, time your message immediately after a major success moment (such as a +3.5x ROI campaign milestone). Congratulate them on their results, ask for a 2-sentence written quote or 60-second video review, and supply guided prompts ("What was your main challenge before working with us?"). Questions

testimonial request email builder FAQ

When is the best time to send a testimonial request email?

Send the request immediately after a major success moment, such as a successful product launch, high ROI report, or positive CSAT survey.

How can I make giving a testimonial easy for clients?

Provide 3 guided questions or draft a 2-sentence draft quote for them to review, edit, and approve with a single click.

Should businesses offer incentives for testimonials?

Small courtesy gifts ($25 Amazon/Starbucks gift cards or account credit) can boost response rates, but ensure compliance with FTC review guidelines.

What guided questions produce the best testimonials?

Ask: 1) What was your primary challenge before using our service? 2) What specific quantitative results did you achieve? 3) What would you tell someone considering us?

What is the ideal length for a testimonial request email?

Keep the email under 200 words so clients can read the request in 30 seconds.

Can I draft a testimonial quote for the client to approve?

Yes! Offering a pre-written draft quote based on their past feedback is often appreciated by busy executives.
