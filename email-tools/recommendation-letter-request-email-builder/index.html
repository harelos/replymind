Expert Tip

Recommenders—especially university professors and C-level executives—often write dozens of recommendation letters per year. Providing a 1-page "brag sheet" outlining your specific project achievements, grades, and a customizable letter draft reduces their workload by 80% and ensures your letter highlights precise metrics rather than generic praise.

The P.R.A.I.S.E. Framework

The P.R.A.I.S.E. Framework provides students and professionals seeking letters from University Professors, Chief Technology Officers, and Senior Research Directors with a structured outline. Purpose & opportunity (stating the application target), Relationship recap (reminding them of your top projects), Attached brag sheet (providing resume and bulleted metrics), Information & portal links (giving explicit submission steps), Scheduled deadline cushion (offering a 3-4 week runway), and Express heartfelt gratitude (thanking them for their time and mentorship).

Best practices for requesting letters of recommendation

Securing an impactful letter of recommendation requires thoughtful preparation and extreme respect for your recommender's calendar. Whether you are applying to competitive MBA programs evaluated by Department Heads, seeking executive leadership roles endorsed by a former Chief Technology Officer, or pursuing fellowships with Executive Sponsors, your request email dictates the strength of your letter.

Academic and graduate admissions data shows that recommendation requests accompanied by a pre-structured brag sheet and bulleted project list are completed 52% faster and receive higher strength-of-endorsement scores. Busy leaders appreciate receiving concrete facts and figures they can insert straight into their recommendation templates.

Questions

recommendation letter request email builder FAQ

How far in advance should I request a letter of recommendation?

Give recommenders at least 3 to 4 weeks advance notice before the submission deadline so they can write a thoughtful letter without stress.

Should I offer to write a draft of the recommendation letter?

Yes. Offering a preliminary draft or bulleted brag sheet saves the recommender time and ensures all key metrics and achievements are included.

What documents should I attach to a recommendation request email?

Attach your updated resume, transcript or portfolio, targeted job/program description, and a 1-page summary of your key accomplishments.

Is it appropriate to ask a professor or executive for a recommendation by email?

Yes, email is the standard professional method. It allows you to attach all necessary background documents and submission instructions clearly.

What if my recommender misses the submission deadline?

Send a gentle follow-up email 1 week before the deadline expressing gratitude and asking if they need any additional background information.

How do I thank someone after they write a recommendation letter?

Send an immediate written thank-you email upon submission, and follow up later with a handwritten note or small gift once you receive your acceptance outcome.
