A customer feedback survey email is a CX research message sent to active users or purchasers requesting participation in a satisfaction survey, Net Promoter Score (NPS) poll, or product interview. Writing a high-converting survey email requires communicating the brief completion time (e.g., "2 minutes"), explaining how feedback directly shapes future product improvements, and providing a direct survey CTA. Best practice stresses timing outreach post-onboarding or post-purchase.

Questions

customer feedback survey email FAQ

How long should a customer feedback survey take?

Keep surveys between 1 and 3 questions total, taking under 2 minutes to complete.

How can companies increase email survey response rates?

State 'Takes under 2 minutes' in the subject line, embed 1-click NPS rating buttons directly in the email body, and offer a small sweepstakes entry.

What is the difference between CSAT and NPS survey emails?

CSAT measures satisfaction with a specific support interaction, while NPS measures overall brand loyalty and recommendation likelihood.

When is the best time to send customer feedback survey emails?

Send CSAT surveys within 1 hour of ticket resolution; send NPS surveys 30 to 60 days after account onboarding.

Should feedback survey emails offer gift card incentives?

Offering entry into a $100 gift card draw can boost completion rates by up to 28% for longer research surveys.

How should negative feedback from surveys be handled?

Trigger automated alerts for low scores (NPS 0-6) so account managers can follow up within 24 hours to resolve issues.
