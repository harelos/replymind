Questions

job application follow up email FAQ

How long should I wait to follow up after applying for a job?

Wait 5 to 10 business days after submitting your resume before sending your first follow-up email.

Who should I send my job application follow-up email to?

Send it to the recruiter, talent acquisition specialist, or hiring manager named in the job posting or portal.

What subject line works best when checking application status?

Use specific subjects like "Following up - Senior Product Designer application - [Your Name]".

Should I attach my resume to the follow-up email again?

Yes. Re-attaching your updated resume saves the recruiter time and makes reviewing your candidacy frictionless.

How many follow-ups should I send if I don't receive a reply?

Send 1 to 2 polite follow-ups spaced 7 business days apart. If no response arrives, focus on other open opportunities.
