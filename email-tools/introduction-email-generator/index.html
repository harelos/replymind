Why it matters

Clear enough to act on. Human enough to trust.

The purpose of an introduction is not to demonstrate how much you know about both people. It is to create enough relevant context for them to decide what happens next.

Avoid sharing sensitive business or personal information without permission.

Questions

professional introduction email generator FAQ

What is a double opt-in introduction?

You ask each person privately whether they want the introduction before connecting them.

Should the introducer stay copied?

Usually only for the initial handoff. The recipients can move you to BCC or start a new thread.

How much context should I include?

Enough to explain the relevance and desired next step—usually two to four sentences.
