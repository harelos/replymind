# ReplyMind funnel content map

Audience: consultants, agency owners, recruiters, account managers, and service operators whose replies affect revenue, trust, or momentum.

## Shared promise

ReplyMind helps a person turn a clear intention into a draft inside the conversation they are already handling. The person reviews, edits, and sends it.

## Story advertorial

1. Reply pressure: a reply can be short and still demand several decisions.
2. Tension scene: choose tone, outcome, and context without losing the thread.
3. Demonstration: real Gmail intent picker and draft.
4. Voice: local sample analysis and derived profile.
5. Context: local contact note, used only when requested.
6. Follow-up: a reminder created by the person.
7. Plain privacy explanation.
8. Transition: see the full workflow and plans.

CTA: `See how it handles a real reply` → sales page.

## Seven reasons

1. Works where replies happen.
2. Starts with the job of the reply.
3. Begins from the person's writing samples.
4. Keeps contact context deliberate.
5. Keeps follow-ups visible.
6. Leaves the send decision with the person.
7. Lets the person try the core workflow first.

Each reason has its own visual placement. CTAs follow reasons 2, 5, and 7.
