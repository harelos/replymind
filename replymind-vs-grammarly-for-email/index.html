Can I use ReplyMind together with Grammarly in Chrome?

Yes! Many users use ReplyMind to generate full email drafts in seconds, and keep Grammarly active as a secondary proofreader if desired.

Why is ReplyMind faster than Grammarly for replying to emails?

Grammarly requires you to write the first draft yourself before suggesting rewrites. ReplyMind analyzes the incoming thread and drafts the entire response automatically based on a simple intent click.
